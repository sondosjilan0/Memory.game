Memory Challenge

What just happened?

  • You tried to hold 12 symbols in working memory. Most people can keep about four items in mind at once (Cowan, 2001), so most answers came from what you looked at and linked together.
  • Attention decides what gets stored. A symbol you never looked at could not be recalled, however long the timer ran.
  • Action games exercise this skill. Players track many moving targets and switch focus fast. Green and Bavelier (2003) found action gamers beat non-gamers on visual attention tasks, though how far the benefit carries over to everyday life is still debated.
  • The same design also captures attention. Short loops, rewards and notifications shape what players do next. That is the link to the rest of this talk.
